Zero Division - installable app

To put it on your phone's home screen:
1. Host this folder on any HTTPS site. The quickest free way:
   - Unzip this file.
   - Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop on a computer and drag the unzipped "zero-division" folder onto the page.
   - Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
2. Open that link on your phone:
   - iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
   - Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3. Launch it from the home screen icon. It opens fullscreen in landscape.
   After the first load it also works offline.
